Print one, stick it near your workspace, tick the boxes. They print in the language you have selected (EN or FIL).

DailyAraw-araw

  1. Issue an invoice for every sale that needs one (non-VAT: ₱500 or more, or below ₱500 if the buyer asks; VAT-registered: every sale).
  2. Don’t give only an official receipt when an invoice is required.
  3. Ask for an invoice whenever you buy something for the business.
  4. Write on each expense document what it was for.
  5. Put today’s documents in this month’s folder (scan thermal receipts).
  6. Keep business and personal money separate.
  7. Record today’s transactions in the journal (or at least before the week ends).
  1. Mag-issue ng invoice sa bawat bentang nangangailangan nito (non-VAT: ₱500 pataas, o mas mababa sa ₱500 kung humingi ang buyer; VAT-registered: bawat benta).
  2. Huwag official receipt lang ang ibigay kapag invoice ang kailangan.
  3. Humingi ng invoice tuwing may binibili para sa negosyo.
  4. Isulat sa bawat dokumento ng gastos kung para saan ito.
  5. Ilagay ang mga dokumento ngayong araw sa folder ng buwan (i-scan ang resibong thermal).
  6. Paghiwalayin ang pera ng negosyo at personal.
  7. I-record sa journal ang mga transaksyon ngayong araw (o kahit bago matapos ang linggo).

MonthlyBuwanan

Month:Buwan: ____________________

  1. File all documents for the month: invoices issued, expense invoices/receipts, 2307s, bank and e-wallet statements.
  2. Check that every document has a journal entry, and every entry has a document.
  3. Total the journals. Debits = credits?
  4. Post to the General Ledger and update balances.
  5. Reconcile cash: ledger balance vs cash on hand + bank + e-wallet.
  6. Write down this month’s gross sales/receipts: ₱__________
  7. Write down this month’s total expenses: ₱__________
  8. Back up this month’s digital files.
  1. I-file ang lahat ng dokumento ng buwan: mga invoice na in-issue, invoice/resibo ng gastos, mga 2307, bank at e-wallet statements.
  2. I-check na may journal entry ang bawat dokumento, at may dokumento ang bawat entry.
  3. I-total ang mga journal. Pantay ba ang debit at credit?
  4. I-post sa General Ledger at i-update ang mga balance.
  5. I-reconcile ang cash: balance sa ledger vs cash na hawak + bangko + e-wallet.
  6. Isulat ang gross sales/receipts ngayong buwan: ₱__________
  7. Isulat ang kabuuang gastos ngayong buwan: ₱__________
  8. I-backup ang mga digital file ng buwan.

QuarterlyKada quarter

Quarter:Quarter: ____________________

  1. Finish the monthly checklist for all three months.
  2. Gross sales/receipts for the quarter (full invoice amounts, including amounts withheld): ₱__________
  3. Total expenses for the quarter: ₱__________
  4. Collect all 2307s for the quarter. Total withheld: ₱__________
  5. List and follow up any missing 2307s.
  6. Using the Optional Standard Deduction? Still keep your gross sales records.
  7. Keep a one-page quarter summary with your records.
  1. Tapusin ang buwanang checklist para sa tatlong buwan.
  2. Gross sales/receipts ng quarter (buong halaga ng invoice, kasama ang kinaltas): ₱__________
  3. Kabuuang gastos ng quarter: ₱__________
  4. Tipunin ang lahat ng 2307 ng quarter. Kabuuang kinaltas: ₱__________
  5. Ilista at i-follow up ang mga kulang na 2307.
  6. Optional Standard Deduction ba ang gamit mo? Itago pa rin ang records ng gross sales.
  7. Gumawa ng isang pahinang summary ng quarter at itago sa records.

YearlyTaunan

Year:Taon: ____________________

  1. Finish the quarterly checklist for all four quarters.
  2. Total each ledger account for the year and check debits = credits (trial balance).
  3. Box and label the year’s 12 monthly folders; store them with the year’s books.
  4. Back up all digital records in two places and check the files open.
  5. Keep books, invoices and supporting documents for 5 years. Check before discarding anything older.
  6. Books for the coming year: registration deadline for manual and loose-leaf books is [To confirm].
  7. Do your books or financial statements need a CPA audit? [To confirm]
  8. Check if the ₱500 invoice threshold has been adjusted (it is adjusted every 3 years based on the Consumer Price Index).
  9. Still have leftover ORs? Make sure they’re stamped or converted.
  1. Tapusin ang quarterly checklist para sa apat na quarter.
  2. I-total ang bawat ledger account para sa taon at i-check kung pantay ang debit at credit (trial balance).
  3. Ilagay sa kahon at lagyan ng label ang 12 buwanang folder; itabi kasama ng libro ng taon.
  4. I-backup ang lahat ng digital records sa dalawang lugar at i-check kung nabubuksan.
  5. Itago ang libro, invoice at supporting documents nang 5 taon. I-check muna bago itapon ang mas luma.
  6. Libro para sa susunod na taon: ang deadline ng registration para sa manual at loose-leaf ay [To confirm].
  7. Kailangan bang i-audit ng CPA ang libro o financial statements mo? [To confirm]
  8. I-check kung na-adjust na ang ₱500 na invoice threshold (ina-adjust ito kada 3 taon base sa Consumer Price Index).
  9. May natira pang OR? Siguraduhing natatakan o na-convert na.

💡 Why it mattersBakit ito mahalaga?

Habits beat willpower. A checklist you can see every day turns bookkeeping into a routine instead of a once-a-quarter panic.

Mas maaasahan ang habit kaysa sa sipag. Kapag nakikita mo araw-araw ang checklist, nagiging routine ang bookkeeping imbes na pagkataranta tuwing quarter.

⚠️ Common mistakesMga karaniwang pagkakamali

  • Printing the checklist and never looking at it again — put it where you work.
  • Ticking boxes without actually doing the step.
  • Skipping the yearly backup check until a file won’t open.
  • Ipina-print ang checklist pero hindi na tinitingnan — idikit kung saan ka nagtatrabaho.
  • Nagti-tsek nang hindi talaga ginagawa ang hakbang.
  • Nilalaktawan ang taunang pag-check ng backup hanggang may file na hindi na mabuksan.
