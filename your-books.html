Your booksAng mga libro mo

“Books” sounds scary, but there are really just two ideas: a journal and a ledger. Same information, organized two ways.

Nakakatakot pakinggan ang “books,” pero dalawang ideya lang talaga ito: ang journal at ang ledger. Parehong impormasyon, dalawang paraan ng pag-aayos.

Journal vs ledgerJournal vs ledger

📖 Journal = your diaryJournal = diary mo

The journal is the “book of original entry.” You write every transaction in date order, as it happens: the date, which accounts changed, the debit and credit amounts, and a short explanation.

Ang journal ang “book of original entry” (dito unang isinusulat ang transaksyon). Isinusulat mo ang bawat transaksyon ayon sa petsa, habang nangyayari: petsa, aling account ang nagbago, halaga ng debit at credit, at maikling paliwanag.

🗂️ Ledger = sorted by accountLedger = naka-grupo ayon sa account

The General Ledger has one page per account — Cash, Service Income, Internet Expense, and so on. You copy (“post”) each journal amount to its account page, so you can see each account’s running balance.

Ang General Ledger (ang “master list” ng bawat account mo – cash, benta, gastos) ay may isang page kada account — Cash, Service Income, Internet Expense, atbp. Kinokopya (“pino-post”) mo ang bawat halaga mula sa journal papunta sa page ng account nito, para makita ang running balance ng bawat account.

💡 Why it mattersBakit ito mahalaga?

  • The journal tells the story in order; the ledger tells you where you stand. You need both.
  • Registered books with the QR Code Stamp show they’re your official books.
  • Picking the right format at the start saves you from redoing your records later.
  • Sa journal mo makikita ang kuwento ayon sa pagkakasunod; sa ledger mo malalaman kung nasaan ka na. Kailangan mo pareho.
  • Ang rehistradong libro na may QR Code Stamp ang nagpapakitang ito ang opisyal mong libro.
  • Kapag tama ang napili mong format sa umpisa, hindi mo na kailangang ulitin ang records mo.

⚠️ Common mistakesMga karaniwang pagkakamali

  • Registering books, then leaving them blank until filing season.
  • Forgetting to attach the QR Code Stamp to the first page.
  • Keeping a second “unofficial” notebook with different numbers.
  • Using loose-leaf pages without getting a Permit to Use or binding them.
  • Writing in the journal but never posting to the ledger, so you never know your balances.
  • Nag-register ng libro, tapos iniwang blangko hanggang filing season.
  • Nakalimutang idikit ang QR Code Stamp sa unang pahina.
  • May pangalawang “unofficial” na notebook na iba ang mga numero.
  • Gumagamit ng loose-leaf nang walang Permit to Use o hindi pinapa-bind.
  • Nagsusulat sa journal pero hindi nagpo-post sa ledger, kaya hindi mo alam ang mga balance mo.
