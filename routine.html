If it doesn’t match, check theseKung hindi tugma, tingnan ang mga ito
  • A bank charge, transfer fee or interest you haven’t recorded.
  • A payment received or made that’s missing from the journal.
  • An amount typed with swapped digits (₱1,690 vs ₱1,960). If the difference divides evenly by 9, this is a likely cause.
  • Personal money mixed into the business account.
  • Bank charge, transfer fee o interest na hindi pa naire-record.
  • Bayad na natanggap o ibinayad na wala sa journal.
  • Halagang nagkapalit ang digit (₱1,690 vs ₱1,960). Kung ang diperensya ay eksaktong nahahati sa 9, malamang ito ang dahilan.
  • Personal na perang nahalo sa business account.

💡 Why it mattersBakit ito mahalaga?

  • Small monthly check-ups catch mistakes while you still remember what happened.
  • If every month is closed, quarter-end is just adding three numbers.
  • Reconciling cash is the best way to spot a missing entry.
  • Sa maliit na buwanang check-up, nahuhuli ang mali habang naaalala mo pa ang nangyari.
  • Kung sarado na ang bawat buwan, pagsasama na lang ng tatlong numero ang quarter-end.
  • Ang pag-reconcile ng cash ang pinakamagandang paraan para makita ang nawawalang entry.

⚠️ Common mistakesMga karaniwang pagkakamali

  • Skipping month-end and doing all three months at once.
  • Using the cash received instead of gross sales/receipts (forgetting withheld amounts).
  • Ignoring a small cash difference — small differences often point to a missing entry.
  • Not following up missing 2307s until it’s too late.
  • Nilalaktawan ang month-end at sabay-sabay ginagawa ang tatlong buwan.
  • Ang cash na natanggap ang ginagamit imbes na gross sales/receipts (nakakalimutan ang kinaltas).
  • Binabalewala ang maliit na diperensya sa cash — madalas, senyales ito ng nawawalang entry.
  • Hindi agad nafo-follow up ang kulang na 2307.
