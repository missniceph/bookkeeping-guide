Cheat sheet: which side makes an account go UP?Cheat sheet: saang side TUMATAAS ang account?

Debit — Drawings, Expenses, Assets (Cash, Equipment, Creditable Withholding Tax). Credit — Liabilities, Owner’s Capital, Income. The opposite side makes them go down.

Debit — Drawings, Expenses, Assets (Cash, Equipment, Creditable Withholding Tax). Credit — Liabilities, Owner’s Capital, Income. Sa kabilang side naman sila bumababa.

💡 Why it mattersBakit ito mahalaga?

  • Reading about debits and credits is one thing; doing ten entries makes it click.
  • You’ll see how one entry flows from the journal to the ledger to the month-end totals — the same path your real numbers take to your return.
  • Iba ang nagbabasa lang tungkol sa debit at credit; kapag sampung entry na ang nagawa mo, magki-click na ito.
  • Makikita mo kung paano dumadaloy ang isang entry mula journal papuntang ledger papuntang month-end totals — parehong daan na tinatahak ng totoong numero mo papunta sa return.

⚠️ Common mistakes in this labMga karaniwang pagkakamali sa lab na ito

  • Treating Ana’s own ₱30,000 as income (it’s capital).
  • Treating the ₱12,000 tablet as an expense (it’s equipment).
  • Recording only ₱19,000 income from the client who withheld tax (income is ₱20,000).
  • Treating the ₱5,000 withdrawal as an expense (it’s drawings).
  • Itinuring na kita ang sariling ₱30,000 ni Ana (capital iyon).
  • Itinuring na gastos ang ₱12,000 na tablet (equipment iyon).
  • ₱19,000 lang ang ni-record na kita mula sa client na nagkaltas ng buwis (₱20,000 ang kita).
  • Itinuring na gastos ang ₱5,000 na kinuha ni Ana (drawings iyon).
